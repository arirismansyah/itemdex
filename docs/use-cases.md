# ITEMDEX: Daily Use Cases

Competition: [Google Cloud AI Builder Cup 2026](https://aibuildercup.com/) (Hack2skill, JAPAC).
Theme: start with a real-world problem and build a prototype on Google Cloud that could scale globally.

ITEMDEX turns a photo or video of an item into a card with its identity, size, weight,
condition, second-hand value and last-seen location (place name + GPS).

## Use cases

| # | Use case | How ITEMDEX helps |
|---|----------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Declutter and resell** | Scan a closet or garage, get a resale price per item, sort by value to see what to list first. |
| 2 | **Home inventory for insurance** | Timestamped, geotagged catalogue of belongings with values, useful after theft or fire. |
| 3 | **"Where did I leave it?"** | Search by name, tag or location. Each card keeps a location history. |
| 4 | **Moving house** | Count items and estimate size and weight for a mover. |
| 5 | **Collectors** | Catalogue sneakers, books, toys or gadgets with rarity and condition. |
| 6 | **Small businesses and market sellers** | Quick stock intake without typing, plus pricing suggestions. |
| 7 | **Shared equipment** | Track tools or gear in a workshop, office or lab and where each item was last seen. |
| 8 | **Estate and inheritance** | Value a relative's belongings. |
| 9 | **Kids** | The Pokémon-card framing makes tidying up a game. |

## Recommended pitch angle

The strongest "real-world problem, scalable globally" story is the **second-hand economy and
personal asset inventory** (use cases 1, 2 and 4): resale value, insurance and moving.
The collectible-card style is a good hook but a weaker problem statement on its own.
